⚠️ Offline Emergency Settlement & SOS Guide

You are currently offline or experiencing network disconnection. These statutory emergency protocols and emergency contacts are cached locally on your device for immediate access.

RTA 2006, s. 24 & Criminal Code s. 430

1. Unlawful Lockout & Eviction Defense

  1. Call Police Non-Emergency Immediately: State: "I am a lawful tenant locked out by my landlord without a Sheriff's eviction notice. Under Ontario RTA s. 24, this is an unlawful eviction."
  2. Do Not Sign Anything: Never sign an N11 (Agreement to End Tenancy) under coercion.
  3. Contact Rental Housing Enforcement Unit (RHEU): Call 1-888-772-9277 (Mon-Fri) for immediate provincial inspector intervention.
  4. Emergency LTB Motion: File an emergency Form T2 (Tenant Rights) at the Landlord and Tenant Board requesting an interim lockout order.
Department of Employment and Social Development Act (DESDA)

2. Stolen or Compromised SIN (Social Insurance Number)

  1. File Police Report: Obtain an official police report case number documenting identity theft.
  2. Contact Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (CAFC): Report online or call 1-888-495-8501.
  3. Credit Bureau Fraud Alerts: Immediately contact both credit bureaus to place a 6-year fraud warning:
    • Equifax Canada: 1-800-465-7166
    • TransUnion Canada: 1-800-663-9980
  4. Service Canada Appointment: Visit your nearest Service Canada centre with your police report, primary photo ID, and immigration document to request a replacement/investigation.
Occupational Health and Safety Act (OHSA), s. 43, 50

3. Unsafe Workplace Emergency Refusal

  1. Inform Supervisor Immediately: State clearly: "I am exercising my statutory right to refuse unsafe work under OHSA s. 43 because this condition is dangerous to my health or safety."
  2. Remain in Safe Area: Do not leave the work site; wait in an adjacent safe area while the supervisor investigates in the presence of the JHSC worker representative.
  3. Ministry of Labour Inspector: If the employer does not remedy the hazard, call the Ministry of Labour 24/7 hotline at 1-877-202-0008.
  4. Strict Anti-Reprisal Shield: Under OHSA s. 50, employers are strictly prohibited from firing, suspending, or threatening workers for safety refusals.
Canada Health Act & Community Health Centres Act

4. Free Urgent Care for Uninsured Newcomers

  • Community Health Centres (CHCs): Provide primary medical care, urgent walk-in care, and prenatal care regardless of health card status or immigration documentation.
  • Hospital Emergency Rooms: Under Canadian provincial hospital policies, emergency medical care cannot be denied based on lack of insurance (payment plans/compassionate waivers apply).
  • Interim Federal Health Program (IFHP): Refugee claimants and protected persons receive 100% covered emergency, hospital, and prescription drug benefits under Medavie Blue Cross.
